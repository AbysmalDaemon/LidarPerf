# Security Policy

LidarPerf is pre-alpha and does not yet have a stable release line.

For security-sensitive reports that should not be public, contact the maintainer at `a.dasgupta2002@gmail.com` rather than opening a public issue.

Benchmark adapters may eventually execute third-party commands, containers, and research software. Treat untrusted benchmark definitions and external estimator images as untrusted code. LidarPerf will not claim to sandbox malicious estimators.
